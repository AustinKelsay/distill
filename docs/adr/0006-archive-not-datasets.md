# DISTILL ends at a searchable archive; datasets and export are out

Distilling, training and analysis are later consumers of the archive, not part of it. The earlier build's dataset recipes, redaction at export, approval gate, publishing, recipe proposals, git outcome joins and agent-repaired parsers are removed. They were built before any use had been chosen, and the owner chose to keep the use open.

A future consumer reads through the same read path as every other reader.
